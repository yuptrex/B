This scene scales its raymarching cost (primary steps × noise octaves × self-shadow steps × supersampling) to your device automatically, then lets you push it further with the sliders, up to a new "Molten" ceiling (700 steps, 20 noise octaves, 96 shadow samples, 3.2x supersampling per pixel). Heavier settings = higher GPU load, not a hard requirement — it will still run on modest hardware at a reduced frame rate rather than refusing to load, thanks to gentle auto-adaptive resolution.

WebGL2 not available

This scene needs WebGL2 to run its raymarched shader. Your browser or GPU driver doesn't expose it here — try an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, or Firefox with hardware acceleration enabled.
